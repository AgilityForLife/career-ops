## Role Summary

Amazon is hiring a Program Manager for Delivery Quality & Process Integration within the North America Grocery Delivery organization (Sub Same Day Grocery / SSD-G). The role serves as the central point of contact on the delivery team for driving initiatives that streamline upstream operations processes critical to grocery delivery outcomes. The PM bridges the gap between station operations, fulfillment processes, and last mile delivery — ensuring upstream workflows are optimized to support delivery timeliness, order quality, and SLA compliance. Located in New York City.

Key responsibilities include owning identification of virtual-physical mismatches that create delivery failures, monitoring order damage and quality metrics, driving accountability for SLA compliance across partner teams, and tracking KPIs across the end-to-end delivery pipeline. Salary range: $81,600-$142,800 (NYC). Requires 5+ years of program/project management experience.
