## Role Summary

Citi is hiring a Program Manager (Job Req ID 22464431) based in New York, NY or Irving, TX. The Senior Program Manager accomplishes results through the management of professional team(s) and department(s), managing one or more teams. Responsibilities include assessing creation and signoff of program plan and charter, benefits management plan, stakeholder management plan, acceptance plan, and central program issue log. The role ensures program plans meet business needs as described in program initiation documents, that all stakeholders are identified and included in scope definition activities, and that stakeholders understand the program schedule and key milestones. Works with Program Sponsor to ensure business case/cost benefit analysis aligns with business objectives. Requires in-depth understanding of how areas collectively integrate within the subfunction and contribute to objectives of the entire function. Developed communication and diplomacy skills are required to guide, influence, and convince others.
