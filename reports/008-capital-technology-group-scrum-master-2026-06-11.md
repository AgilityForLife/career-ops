## Role Summary
Capital Technology Group is hiring a Scrum Master for a USCIS identity and access management program, operating in two-week sprint cycles. The role involves facilitating Agile ceremonies, partnering with product owners, business analysts, engineers, QA, DevOps, and program leadership. Required certifications include CSM, PMI-ACP, CSP, or SAFe, with PMP and Lean/Six Sigma preferred. Salary range is $110K-$140K with remote and hybrid options.

## Scoring Rationale
Four core strengths are engaged, but critically CS-2 (Cloud & Data Platform) and CS-3 (DevOps Governance) — two of the candidate's strongest differentiators — are not leveraged by this role. The role is fundamentally a Scrum Master position rather than a Senior TPM or Delivery Lead, which represents a potential title and scope reduction. However, the federal/regulated context (CS-7), strong stakeholder alignment requirement (CS-5), and direct certification match (CS-6) provide solid fit on the engaged dimensions. The 3.6 score reflects the tension between good cultural and certification fit versus underutilization of the candidate's highest-value technical delivery strengths.

## Key Strengths Match

- PMP (preferred qualification) is already held
- Cross-functional stakeholder management experience across engineering, QA, and business teams is a direct match

- Title step-down risk: moving from Senior TPM to Scrum Master could affect future positioning

For C2C conversion, this range would translate to approximately $55-$70/hr, which meets the $60+/hr C2C target at the midpoint and above. Compensation is aligned but not exceptional given the candidate's seniority level and certifications.

Consider applying only if the role has growth potential into a program-level position, or if pipeline volume is low and this serves as a reliable bridge opportunity.
