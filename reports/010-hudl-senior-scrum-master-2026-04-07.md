## Role Summary

Hudl is a sports technology company building products for teams at the high school, club, and small college level (Competitive business unit). The Senior Scrum Master role focuses on coaching and empowering product development teams using Agile frameworks (Lean, Waterfall, Kanban, Scrum), navigating risks and dependencies, removing impediments, mentoring other Scrum Masters, and leading Agile practice initiatives across the company. The role supports hybrid/remote teams and offers flexible work arrangements.

This is a coaching-first Scrum Master position in a non-regulated sports tech environment. While the title aligns with a primary target role, the day-to-day responsibilities center on Agile coaching and facilitation rather than enterprise technical program delivery.
