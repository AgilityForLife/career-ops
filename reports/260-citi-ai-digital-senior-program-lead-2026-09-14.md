## Role Summary

Citi is hiring an AI Digital Senior Program Lead within Global Services at the SVP (Senior Vice President / C14) level in New York, NY. Sitting within the Services Program Management & Execution team, this role drives the structured delivery of intelligent digital experiences used by millions of clients worldwide to manage transactions, accounts, and portfolios. Key responsibilities include: leading end-to-end delivery of multiple AI and digital transformation programs across Global Services ensuring all initiatives meet defined milestones, timelines, and business objectives; establishing and managing a robust program governance framework aligned to the global delivery model; identifying, assessing, and actively managing program risks and issues; partnering closely with Product Management, Operations, and Technology teams to ensure continuous alignment between AI digital program delivery and the overall Services strategy; and owning program budget management across AI and digital initiatives, tracking expenditure against plan and ensuring cost efficiency.

Qualifications include: PMI PMP certification or equivalent program management qualification (required); experience delivering programs across both Agile and Waterfall methodologies with flexibility to apply the right approach to AI and technology-driven program contexts; familiarity with AI-powered digital platforms, client-facing technology products, or intelligent automation in a financial services environment; and knowledge of treasury services, securities, or transaction banking. Compensation: base $163,600-$245,400 (alternate data: $176,720-$265,080) plus discretionary bonus, equity, and full benefits.
