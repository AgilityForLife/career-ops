## Gaps — where he loses

- **Public Trust clearance requirement.** The candidate does not currently hold a Public Trust clearance. While Public Trust is the lowest level of federal personnel vetting (not a security clearance per se), the "ability to obtain" requirement means the candidate must be clearance-eligible. This is typically achievable for US citizens/residents with clean backgrounds, but adds timeline risk (weeks to months for adjudication) and could be a screening gate if the client needs someone cleared immediately.
- **Change management emphasis.** The "Change Manager" portion of the title signals organizational change management (OCM) — stakeholder impact analysis, communication plans, training plans, resistance management. The candidate's CV does not explicitly document OCM methodology certification (Prosci, ADKAR, etc.) or dedicated OCM project experience. The Verisk SAFe transformation has OCM elements but was framed as Agile transformation, not formal OCM.
- **Comp uncertainty and potential floor risk.** No salary posted. IBM PM market data ($93K-$133K) suggests the range could dip close to or below the $100K FTE floor. Federal contract PM roles sometimes have lower comp than commercial PM roles at comparable scope. This is a risk, not a confirmed deal-breaker.
- **"Project Manager" title (not "Program Manager" or "TPM").** Single-project PM framing may understate the candidate's program-level capabilities and could signal a smaller scope than desired.
- **No specific cloud/data technology mentioned** in the JD — the candidate's AWS/Snowflake differentiators may not land as strongly in a generically-framed technology transition role.

## Decision

**PREPARE ONLY.** The core delivery mechanics fit (technology transition management in regulated environments) is genuine, and fully remote is a significant positive. However, three uncertainties warrant caution: (1) Public Trust clearance timeline and whether "ability to obtain" is a soft or hard gate at application stage, (2) comp range may dip close to the $100K floor without the upside of financial services roles, and (3) the change management emphasis requires positioning work to bridge the gap between the candidate's Agile transformation experience and formal OCM methodology. Recommend: prepare a tailored resume with change management framing, apply, and clarify comp and clearance timeline early in the process. If comp comes in below $100K, withdraw.
