## Fit — where he wins

- **Location: perfect.** Hybrid NJ/NY area or Remote — matches Erick's NJ base and remote preference exactly. No relocation or commute friction.
- **Comp: $110,000-$175,000.** Clears the $100K FTE floor. The upper band ($175K) exceeds the $130-150K target range. Strong comp signal.
- **Insurance = regulated industry.** While Erick has no direct insurance experience, the regulated-environment thread (pharma R&D at Sanofi, financial services compliance at Verisk) demonstrates comfort operating under regulatory constraints. CS-7 is transferable.
- **PMO role fits PM experience.** PMP + PMI-ACP + 15+ years of project/program management experience far exceed the requirements. Process creation, documentation, and methodology governance are well within Erick's skillset.
- **Cross-functional stakeholder work.** Working with key leaders and functional areas maps directly to CS-5 (Sanofi US+EU coordination, Verisk VP-level reporting).

## Gaps — where he loses

- **Traditional PMO, not technical.** No cloud, data, DevOps, or infrastructure delivery signals. This role does not leverage Erick's primary technical differentiators (CS-2, CS-3). The role under-utilizes what makes him stand out in the market.
- **Unclear program scale.** "Multiple projects" does not indicate enterprise-program complexity. Could be mid-scale internal projects, not the 15-17 team, $3M budget scope that defines CS-1. Risk of over-qualification without corresponding comp or growth.
- **Insurance domain is new.** Wholesale brokerage, binding authority, and program management (insurance-specific meaning) are unfamiliar domains. Learning curve on industry terminology, business processes, and regulatory landscape.
- **PMO reporting structure.** Reports to PMO Director — may indicate a governance/process-oriented role rather than delivery ownership. Could feel constraining for someone accustomed to owning end-to-end program delivery.
- **No cloud/data trajectory.** This role does not advance Erick's positioning toward cloud/data platform delivery leadership. Career trajectory risk if the role becomes a long-term commitment.

## Positioning / resume guidance

Lead with CS-1 (enterprise delivery discipline — Verisk program scale, $3M budgets, multi-team coordination) and CS-5 (cross-functional stakeholder alignment — Sanofi US+EU, Verisk VP-level reporting). Add CS-7 angles from pharma (Sanofi R&D governance) and financial services (Verisk compliance-aware delivery) to bridge the regulated-industry requirement.

De-emphasize cloud/data infrastructure depth (CS-2, CS-3) — the role does not ask for it and leading with it may signal misalignment. Emphasize process creation, PM methodology governance, and reporting/monitoring capabilities. PMP + PMI-ACP certifications should be prominent.

Do NOT lead with SAFe/Agile coaching (CS-6) or BTII experience. Frame Verisk and Sanofi as proof of structured PM discipline in complex, regulated environments.
