## Role Summary

AHEAD (formerly ThinkAhead), headquartered in Chicago, builds platforms for digital business by weaving together cloud infrastructure, automation and analytics, and software delivery to help enterprises deliver on digital transformation. They are hiring a Senior Project Manager to plan and coordinate all aspects of technical projects (cloud, data, AI, infrastructure) from initiation through delivery, ensuring service delivery success and customer satisfaction. This is a consulting/professional services role managing client engagements.

The role requires 10-12+ years of progressive IT project management experience including multi-phase engagements in cloud platforms (Azure, AWS, GCP — migrations, modernization, landing zones), data & AI (data platform implementations, analytics/BI, AI/ML initiatives), and application modernization. Responsibilities include coaching/mentoring junior PMs, managing project budgets and financials, executive-ready status reporting, and identifying follow-on business opportunities. PMP and Agile certifications (PMI-ACP, PSM, CSM, SAFe) are a plus.
