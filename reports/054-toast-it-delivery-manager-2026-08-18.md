## Resumen

Toast (restaurant technology platform, NYSE: TOST) seeks an IT Delivery Manager to serve as the strategic execution engine for their IT department. This is a hybrid player-coach role combining strategic program management with hands-on delivery and process optimization. The role owns planning, coordination, and delivery of high-visibility, cross-functional initiatives across IT operations teams and acts as the primary liaison between technical teams and business stakeholders. Remote US. Comp: $111,000-$178,000.

## Core Strength Engagement Test

- **CS-1 Enterprise Technical Program Delivery** — ENGAGED. Cross-functional IT delivery, multi-team initiative ownership, end-to-end execution of complex programs, roadmap tracking. This is the core of the role.
- **CS-2 Cloud & Data Platform Modernization** — NOT ENGAGED. The role is IT operations delivery, not cloud/data platform modernization. No AWS, Snowflake, or infrastructure migration specifics. -0.3.
- **CS-3 DevOps Delivery Governance** — PARTIAL. Process optimization and delivery governance are present, but framed as IT operations improvement rather than DevOps pipeline governance. No mention of CI/CD, release management, or CAB processes.
- **CS-4 Executive Reporting via Power BI** — ENGAGED. Defines success metrics for IT programs, tracks major roadmap items, drives adoption metrics. +0.2.
- **CS-5 Cross-Functional Stakeholder Alignment** — ENGAGED. Primary point of contact between IT domains and external business departments. Tech-to-business liaison role across multiple IT operations teams.
- **CS-6 SAFe SPC6** — NOT MENTIONED. No Agile framework specifics. Neutral.
- **CS-7 Regulated & Compliance-Aware Delivery** — NOT ENGAGED. Restaurant technology platform, no regulated industry indicators.

## Fit -- where he wins

- **Cross-functional IT delivery ownership.** The role demands leading end-to-end execution of complex, multi-team IT initiatives -- this maps directly to Verisk program delivery across 15-17 teams. Erick's experience running cross-functional programs at enterprise scale is the strongest proof point.
- **Tech-to-business stakeholder liaison.** Primary liaison between technical teams and business stakeholders mirrors Verisk VP/Director-level reporting and Sanofi US+EU coordination. CS-5 is well exercised here.
- **Success metrics and reporting.** Defining success metrics for IT programs, tracking roadmap items, and driving adoption aligns with CS-4 Power BI dashboard experience at Verisk and Sanofi.
- **Player-coach model.** Erick's career demonstrates both hands-on delivery and strategic program leadership -- not a pure people-manager profile.
- **Comp clears floor.** $111K-$178K range clears the $100K FTE floor.
- **Remote US** -- no location conflict.

## Gaps -- where he loses

- **IT operations scope vs enterprise program delivery.** This is an IT department delivery manager, not an enterprise-wide technical program manager. The scope is narrower -- internal IT operations rather than product/platform delivery. Erick's Verisk and Sanofi experience is in product and platform delivery, which may overqualify him for IT ops coordination.
- **CS-2 absent.** No cloud/data platform modernization component. Erick's strongest technical differentiator (AWS + Snowflake delivery) has no surface area in this role. This leaves him competing on generic PM credentials where the field is crowded.
- **CS-7 absent.** No regulated industry context. Restaurant tech is not pharma or financial services -- the compliance-aware delivery premium does not apply.
- **No SAFe or Agile framework specifics.** CS-6 SPC6 certification has no leverage point.
- **Potential underleveling.** The title "IT Delivery Manager" and the comp band ($111-178K) suggest a role below Erick's experience ceiling. The player-coach framing may mean significant individual contributor work alongside program management.

## Compensation

$111,000 - $178,000 (Remote US). Clears the $100K FTE floor. Midpoint ($144,500) is within target range but below the $150K+ sweet spot seen in stronger-fit roles. Top of band ($178K) is competitive but would require negotiation leverage that the partial CS engagement makes harder to build.

## Positioning / resume guidance

Per skills-model: open with CS-1 (enterprise delivery discipline -- lead with Verisk multi-team program execution, emphasize cross-functional coordination and roadmap delivery). Follow with CS-4 (success metrics, Power BI dashboards, executive reporting). Frame CS-5 (stakeholder alignment) as tech-to-business liaison experience. De-emphasize cloud/data platform specifics (CS-2) since the role does not call for them. Avoid leading with SAFe/SPC6 -- no leverage here. Keep Verisk infrastructure and DevOps governance in supporting role to show process optimization capability (CS-3 partial alignment).

Do NOT lead with Sanofi pharma governance or regulated delivery -- no resonance with restaurant tech. Frame BTII last per positioning lock.

## Decision

**APPLY w/ caveats.** Toast is a recognizable brand (NYSE: TOST, $4B+ revenue) and the role aligns on CS-1/CS-4/CS-5 fundamentals. However, the 3.2/5 score reflects material gaps: no cloud/data platform differentiator (CS-2), no regulated industry premium (CS-7), and an IT operations scope that may underutilize Erick's enterprise program delivery experience. The comp band is acceptable but not compelling enough to offset the positioning risk.

Worth applying if the pipeline has capacity, but this should not displace applications to roles scoring 3.8+ with stronger CS-2 engagement. Erick competes here on general PM delivery credentials rather than his distinctive technical differentiators -- the field is more crowded at that level.
