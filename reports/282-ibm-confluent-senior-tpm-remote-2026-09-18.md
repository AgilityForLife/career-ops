## Summary

Confluent (now an IBM subsidiary following acquisition) is hiring a Senior Technical Program Manager to focus on developing enablement programs for the Post-Sales team, ensuring technical efficacy, operational alignment, and customer success through knowledge sharing and program management. Responsibilities include defining, planning, and executing large-scale technical programs; tracking status against goals and milestones; managing a program governance model for intake, scoping, and prioritization; and managing a program roadmap. Requirements include 8+ years with strong Customer Success exposure in software/SaaS, a BS in CS/Engineering, and a track record in program management, change management, and cross-functional stakeholder engagement. The role is fully remote in the US. No visa sponsorship available.

**Key concern:** Despite the "Technical Program Manager" title, the core function is post-sales technical enablement -- developing programs that train and enable post-sales teams to drive customer success. This is NOT enterprise infrastructure or platform delivery.
