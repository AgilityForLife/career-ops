## Role Summary

Ardent seeks a Scrum Master to support Agile delivery for a Department of Homeland Security (DHS) program. The role serves as team facilitator ensuring Agile principles and best practices drive team performance, stakeholder collaboration, and incremental delivery of high-value solutions.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Facilitate Scrum events: daily stand-ups, sprint planning, reviews, retrospectives
- Collaborate with Product Owners on backlog management
- Remove impediments and support continuous improvement culture
- Guide Agile teams in mission-focused federal environment

**Requirements:**
- Hands-on experience in Scrum and Kanban methodologies
- Prior experience on federal or DHS-related IT projects strongly preferred
- Advanced Scrum certifications (A-CSM, CSP, or equivalent)
- Experience with Lean or Agile scaling frameworks (LeSS, SAFe)
- Background in software development or technical project delivery
- Bachelor's degree in Business, CS, IS, or related
- Must be willing to undergo government background investigation
