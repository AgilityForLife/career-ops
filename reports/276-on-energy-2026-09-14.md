## Role Summary

ON.energy is a battery energy storage company (Miami-based, Texas operations) that develops and integrates large-scale energy storage systems. They are hiring a Program Manager, AI Enablement to build and run the company's enterprise AI adoption program from the ground up. This is a high-ownership role focused on driving AI tool adoption across the organization — running enablement programs (trainings, onboarding, office hours, internal community channels), coaching users on prompt engineering, curating use cases, facilitating adoption events (prompt-a-thons, demos), and tracking adoption metrics and business impact.

The CTO is the executive sponsor. Required: 5+ years PM experience in technology, proven enablement/change management/training experience, strong knowledge of AI productivity tools (Microsoft Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise, Gemini), and familiarity with Microsoft 365 governance (Entra ID, conditional access, Purview, Defender). The role operates "ahead of fully formed governance, tooling, and staffing."
