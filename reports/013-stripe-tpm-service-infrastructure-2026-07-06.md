### Gaps and mitigation

1. **Developer products / open-source experience (required)** — Hard-ish requirement. Erick's internal DevOps platforms (CI/CD, golden AMIs, container platforms at Verisk) served internal engineering "customers," which is adjacent to a developer-platform mandate. *Mitigation:* frame Verisk infra platforms as internal developer-facing products — "engineers were my customers; I owned the platforms they built on." No OSS proof; do not fabricate.
2. **Framework / library / async-platform authorship** — The role sits close to software-engineering internals (frameworks, async platforms, service libraries). Erick delivers and governs, he does not author frameworks. *Mitigation:* lead with delivery ownership of container/immutable-infra platforms and blue/green; be honest that his lane is program delivery + DevOps governance, not code-level framework design.
3. **Big-Tech seniority bar (L4+ TPM at Stripe)** — Stripe's TPM culture (written-proposal heavy, deeply technical) sets a high bar; Erick's evidence base is enterprise financial-services + pharma, not hyperscaler product infra. *Mitigation:* CS-1 scale (50 engineers, $3M budget, 4 releases/yr) is competitive at L3–L4; anchor there and on quantified governance outcomes.
4. **AI tool-chain fluency (preferred)** — Not on CV. *Mitigation:* mention active AI product-development track as genuine, current, hands-on exposure (do not overstate as enterprise delivery).

**Level detected:** Senior TPM, Stripe L3–L4 equivalent (7+ yrs, autonomous, large-scale cross-org).
**Candidate's natural level for this archetype:** Senior TPM / Delivery Lead — matches on paper.

**Sell senior without lying:**

- Follow with **CS-2 + CS-3**: multi-year AWS/Snowflake modernization roadmap; Snowflake OneMesh migration at Sanofi; 40% fewer outages, 70% faster provisioning, 60% better stability. Migration programs driven to conclusion is exactly what this JD asks for.
- **CS-5** for the "ambiguity + cross-time-zone + consensus" language: US + Europe cross-functional coordination under R&D governance.
- Position financial-services + pharma regulated delivery as a *credibility asset* for a fintech infrastructure company — Stripe is financial infrastructure; Erick has shipped in regulated financial and pharma environments.

**If downleveled:** Stripe L3 median TC ~$300K far exceeds Erick's FTE floor ($100K), so an L3 offer is financially excellent. Accept L3 if offered; negotiate a 6-month review toward L4 with explicit scope-expansion criteria (own a full cross-org migration program end to end).

**Positioning risk:** Do NOT lead with Agile coaching / SAFe / instruction. The JD's one "train them to be better at delivery" line is a *bonus behavior*, not the core function — surface coaching only as "I raise the delivery muscle of teams around me," never as identity. De-emphasis rule does not cap this role (core function is TPM delivery, not Tier-4 coaching).
