## Recommendation

**PREPARE ONLY, low priority**

Compensation and remote logistics are excellent, but the crypto prime dealer domain wall is the central challenge. The candidate has no trading, exchange, cryptocurrency, or fintech background. CS-1 (program delivery) transfers generically, but sFOX will likely prioritize candidates with fintech or trading technology experience.

Only proceed if the candidate is genuinely willing to bridge the fintech gap and is attracted to the crypto/institutional trading domain. Otherwise, the preparation time is better spent on roles with stronger CS engagement.

If proceeding, frame the application around:
1. Enterprise delivery discipline applied to fast-paced environments
2. Verisk financial services background as adjacent (insurance/risk analytics is financial services-adjacent)
3. Willingness to learn the trading/exchange domain quickly
