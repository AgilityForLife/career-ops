## Company Overview

Airtable is a collaborative work management platform that combines the flexibility of a spreadsheet with the structure of a database. The company serves enterprise customers who use the platform to build custom workflows and applications without code. Airtable's Professional Services (PS) team delivers implementation engagements for enterprise clients, bridging the gap between product capability and customer-specific business requirements. The company has grown significantly in the enterprise segment, which drives demand for structured program delivery on the PS side.

## Role Summary

This is a **customer-facing technical delivery lead** within Airtable's Professional Services organization, focused on the East region. The PM owns delivery outcomes across three stakeholder axes: the internal Airtable PS team, external development partners, and enterprise customer stakeholders. Core responsibilities include:

- Driving shared milestones across teams operating at different cadences and compliance requirements
- Running steering committees and managing scope conversations directly with enterprise customers
- Owning customer outcomes end-to-end, not just internal delivery metrics
- Operating hands-on within Airtable as the primary operational tool (platform fluency is mandatory, not optional)
- Navigating compliance requirements and organizational dynamics across multiple customer environments

The role requires someone who has delivered technical programs **for external customers** in a professional services or consulting context — internal enterprise delivery does not satisfy this requirement. AI tool fluency and PMP certification are explicitly called out.

### Gaps

- **CRITICAL: No external customer-facing PS delivery experience.** This is the single largest gap and the primary risk factor. The JD is explicit: "Delivered technical programs for external customers in professional services or consulting context (NOT just internal projects)." Erick's career is built on internal enterprise delivery — leading programs within Verisk, within Sanofi, within BTII. He has not operated as a PS delivery lead where the customer is an external enterprise client paying for implementation services. This is a fundamentally different dynamic: the customer controls the budget, scope negotiations carry revenue implications, delivery failures can trigger contract penalties, and the PM must represent the vendor while serving the client. None of Erick's proof points demonstrate this.
- **Airtable platform fluency is required and absent.** The JD specifies: "Work directly in Airtable as an operational tool — hands-on platform fluency required." This is not a nice-to-have; it is a stated requirement. Erick has no Airtable experience. While tool adoption is learnable, competing candidates who already live in Airtable will have an immediate advantage.
- **No consulting or professional services organizational context.** Beyond the customer-facing gap, the broader PS operating model — utilization targets, engagement scoping, SOW management, billable vs. non-billable time, partner channel coordination — is a distinct discipline. Erick's career has been in enterprise IT and product delivery organizations, not services firms.
- **CS-2 and CS-3 are entirely absent.** The role does not engage Erick's strongest technical differentiators (AWS, Snowflake, DevOps governance). His most compelling proof points — cloud platform delivery, 40% outage reduction, 60% stability improvement — are irrelevant to this role. This leaves him competing on pure program management credentials, where his profile is strong but not uniquely differentiated.

## Recommendation

**HOLD — Do not prioritize.** Score: 3.1/5.

The compensation is attractive and the program management fundamentals align, but the **external customer-facing PS delivery requirement is a structural gap** that cannot be bridged with positioning alone. This is not a transferable-skills argument the hiring team is likely to accept — the JD explicitly distinguishes between internal project delivery and external customer delivery, which means they have already screened out internal-only candidates by design.

The Airtable platform fluency requirement adds a second concrete gap. Combined, these two gaps mean Erick would be competing at a disadvantage against candidates who have both PS delivery backgrounds and Airtable operational experience.

**If the candidate chooses to proceed despite the gaps:**
- Lead with CS-1 (enterprise delivery scale) and CS-5 (cross-functional stakeholder alignment across organizational boundaries)
- Frame the Sanofi engagement as the closest analog to external delivery: cross-geography coordination, R&D governance constraints imposed by the business (not IT), and operating within a structured program framework where the delivery team served internal "customers" with formal requirements
- Emphasize PMP + AI fluency as explicit JD requirements that are checked
- Do not overstate Airtable familiarity — acknowledge it as a ramp-up item and frame tool adoption track record (Jira, Jira Align, Power BI, Snowflake ecosystem) as evidence of rapid platform fluency
- Acknowledge the PS delivery gap directly in the cover letter rather than hoping it goes unnoticed — frame it as "internal enterprise delivery at the scale and complexity that PS engagements demand, ready to bring that discipline to a customer-facing context"

**Bottom line:** The role asks for a specific profile (PS delivery lead with Airtable fluency) that does not match Erick's career trajectory. The score reflects genuine CS-1 and CS-5 engagement tempered by the absence of the role's most distinctive requirement. Apply only if pipeline volume is low and the candidate is willing to invest in a long-shot application.
