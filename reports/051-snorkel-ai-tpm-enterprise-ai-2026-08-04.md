## Company Overview

Snorkel AI is a data-centric AI company founded by Stanford AI Lab researchers. The platform enables enterprises to build, deploy, and manage AI applications by focusing on programmatic data labeling and data development rather than model-centric approaches. Snorkel has raised significant venture funding and serves large enterprise customers across financial services, healthcare, government, and technology sectors. The company operates in a high-growth segment of the AI market, competing with traditional MLOps and data labeling platforms.

Key differentiators: data-centric AI methodology, enterprise customer focus, strong research pedigree (Stanford origins), and an expanding product portfolio that now includes agents, RAG, evaluation frameworks, knowledge bases, classification, and extraction capabilities.
