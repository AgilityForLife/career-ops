### Gaps y mitigación

1. **"SaaS company" experience (soft gap, not blocker).** Verisk and Sanofi are enterprise data platforms, not pure multi-tenant B2B SaaS vendors. *Mitigation:* frame Verisk analytics products as productized data services with continuous release cadence (4 releases/yr, sprint-level deploys), and lead with the transferable core: multi-instance platform migration, cohort sequencing, rollback governance. One cover-letter line: "I've run platform migrations where hundreds of production environments had to cut over with zero data loss and clean rollback paths." Not a hard blocker — the JD's required qualifications center on the migration/framework skill, which is directly held.

2. **"Multi-customer migration" framing (adjacent, coverable).** Erick's migrations were internal-platform (OneMesh) and infra (AWS), not customer-by-customer instance migrations across 600 accounts. *Mitigation:* map "cohort planning" to how he sequenced ARTs/value streams and staged infra cutovers; emphasize the repeatable-framework-to-reduce-per-unit-effort mechanic (70% provisioning cut) which is the exact economic logic the JD wants for "reduce per-customer effort."

3. **Customer Success / customer-lifecycle coordination (nice-to-have, partial).** *Mitigation:* draw on business-stakeholder and UAT/hypercare/transition-to-ops coordination at Sanofi as the analog for customer readiness and communication protocols.

4. **Research-admin / higher-ed / healthcare software (nice-to-have only).** *Mitigation:* Sanofi pharma R&D governance is the closest adjacency — regulated, research-driven data environment. Lead this in any domain-fit question. No fabrication of Cayuse-domain experience.

5. **PgMP (nice-to-have).** Holds PMP + PMI-ACP + SPC6 — PMP satisfies the "PMP, PgMP, or equivalent" clause fully.

No hard blockers. Comp-not-listed is the only verify item.
