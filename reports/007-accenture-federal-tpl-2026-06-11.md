## Scoring Rationale
Five of seven core strengths are engaged, including the two highest-weighted strengths (CS-1 and CS-2). The combination of enterprise-scale delivery, hybrid cloud architecture, DevSecOps governance, and federal regulatory context maps strongly to the candidate's profile. The score is held at 4.0 rather than higher because the HPC/GPU-based AI/ML workload specialization is a domain the candidate has not directly operated in, and the role is classified as a Technical Project Lead rather than a Senior TPM, which may represent a lateral or slight step in title. The absence of CS-4 (Power BI) and CS-6 (SAFe) engagement is minor given the strength of the other five matches.
