## Role Summary

Litmos is an AI-powered learning management platform serving 11,000+ customers with 30 million learners across 150 countries and 37 languages. They are hiring an AI Program Manager to coordinate AI-related projects across internal teams (Finance, Product, Customer Success). This is described as a hands-on, execution-focused role responsible for keeping AI initiatives on track — planning, scheduling, driving projects to completion, and helping teams build AI skills.

Key responsibilities include taking ownership of select projects end-to-end (proposal through execution), identifying process bottlenecks, suggesting improvements to project tracking and coordination, sharing status updates with program leadership, and advancing the organization's shift toward an "agentic-first operating model." Required: 4-5+ years in program/project management, experience managing a portfolio of related projects, familiarity with Jira/Asana/Smartsheet, and basic AI/agentic tools knowledge.
