## Recommendation

**PREPARE ONLY**

This is an aspirational role with exceptional compensation and brand value. CS-1 (enterprise delivery at scale) translates directly — Verisk's 15-17 team coordination across 4 releases/year is legitimate program complexity. CS-5 (cross-functional stakeholder alignment) is the second thesis.

However, the domain gap is real. AI model launches require fluency in research pipelines, model evaluation, safety/alignment considerations, and developer-facing product delivery that the candidate does not currently have. The preparation path should include:

1. Deep study of Anthropic's product launch cadence and public communications
2. Understanding of AI model evaluation and safety frameworks
3. Framing Verisk enterprise releases as analogous to complex product launches with cross-functional dependencies
4. Building a narrative around "delivery complexity transfers, domain is learnable"

NYC 25% hybrid is workable from NJ. Proceed with preparation but manage expectations — the candidate pool for Anthropic TPM roles is extremely competitive.
