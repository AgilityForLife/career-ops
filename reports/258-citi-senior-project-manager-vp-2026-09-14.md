## Role Summary

Citi is hiring a Senior Project Manager at the VP level in New York, NY. The role involves managing an ongoing Book of Work reporting to senior technology management, business heads, and regulators. Responsible for delivery of large cross-organizational initiatives including defining scope, objectives, and requirements with business end users, establishing plans with milestones, assignment of deliverables, and checkpoints. Creates and conducts meetings of key stakeholders, communicating plans and status on a regular basis with all participants and senior management. Tracks progress, identifies issues, and overcomes roadblocks with delivery at organizational scale. Requires a record of delivery as a project manager on large, complex and cross-organizational initiatives, managing multiple stakeholder relationships across trading, risk, compliance, and technology. Specifically requires a deep understanding of at least one area in mortgage trading.
