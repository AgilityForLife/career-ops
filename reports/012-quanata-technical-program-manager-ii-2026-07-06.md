### Gaps y mitigación

**GAP 1 — "5+ years software engineering product development" (HARD-ish blocker).**
- Blocker vs nice-to-have: Reads as a hard filter given the $210–285K band. Quanata likely wants a TPM who has *been* an engineer or shipped product as an engineering IC. Erick has led engineers and owned technical roadmaps, but has not done 5 years of software engineering product development himself.
- Adjacent experience: Yes — owned delivery of golden AMIs, Terraform modules, CI/CD, container platforms; translated business specs into user stories/acceptance criteria; sat inside engineering orgs for 6 years. This is technical depth *at the delivery layer*, not IC engineering.
- Portfolio cover: None that closes a 5-year SWE requirement honestly.
- Mitigation: Reframe as "5+ years embedded in software engineering delivery, owning technical roadmaps and engineering goal-planning across cloud/DevOps/data platforms." Be honest in a cover note: position as a technically-embedded TPM, not a former engineer. If a recruiter treats the SWE line as a strict filter, this is a likely auto-screen-out — do not overstate hands-on coding. **Do not fabricate an engineering title.**

**GAP 2 — Cloud/data stack not explicitly named (CS-2 only adjacent).**
- The JD is telematics/connected-vehicle data-heavy but does not name AWS/Snowflake. Erick's CS-2 is a strong latent match; surface AWS + Snowflake + data-platform delivery aggressively in the summary to convert the adjacency into an explicit hit.

**GAP 3 — "TPM II" title vs premium comp.**
- The "II" plus $285K ceiling suggests Quanata's internal leveling runs high; the real bar is Senior/Staff. Erick's scope (5 teams, $3M, 50+ eng) supports Senior positioning, but the SWE-background expectation is the gate, not the PM scope.
