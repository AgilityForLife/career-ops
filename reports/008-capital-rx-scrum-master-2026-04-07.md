## Role Summary

Capital Rx is a healthcare technology company operating in the pharmacy benefits management (PBM) space. The Scrum Master role is responsible for facilitating Scrum events, coaching a single team on Agile practices, conducting quarterly health checks, supporting onboarding, mediating conflicts, and fostering collaboration. The position requires a Bachelor's Degree or 3 years of work experience and a minimum of 3 years as a Scrum Master. Preferred qualifications include CSM or equivalent certification, CI/CD experience, Lean Startup, Design Thinking, and forecasting/release planning with agile metrics. Location is remote for non-local candidates; hybrid for NYC-local.

This is a team-level Scrum Master position with a low experience threshold (3 years). The scope described is single-team ceremony facilitation and coaching — not enterprise program delivery or multi-team coordination.
