## Scoring Rationale
With 3 of 7 core strengths engaged (CS-1, CS-4 partial, CS-5), the base score lands at 3.2. CS-1 is clearly present through multi-team delivery ownership, and CS-5 is a central requirement. CS-4 gets partial credit for data-driven orientation but lacks explicit BI/dashboard requirements. The role's heavy emphasis on hands-on technical system knowledge (Linear internals, Salesforce workflow design, data pipeline debugging) shifts the profile away from the candidate's enterprise program governance strengths toward a more engineering-adjacent TPM archetype. No cloud/DevOps/SAFe/regulated environment engagement limits the score ceiling.

## Key Strengths Match
- Multi-team delivery coordination across engineering and business stakeholders aligns directly with CS-1
- Cross-functional alignment experience (CS-5) maps well to the Revenue Technology org's need to bridge Product, Engineering, Data, and Revenue
- PMP and structured program management background supports the delivery sequencing and dependency management requirements
- Compensation range ($118K-$148K + equity) meets candidate targets comfortably

## Key Gaps
- Role expects a TPM who can directly unblock engineers at the system level (Linear workflows, Salesforce configurations, data pipeline debugging) — candidate's strength is governance at scale, not hands-on system triage
- No cloud platform, DevOps, or infrastructure delivery component — three of the candidate's strongest differentiators go unused
- No SAFe or scaled agile framework — SPC6 certification provides no advantage
- Fintech context does not leverage the candidate's regulated environment experience (pharma + financial services compliance)
- Mercury's engineering-first culture may undervalue traditional program management credentials in favor of technical depth

## Compensation Analysis
Salary range of $118K-$148K base plus equity and benefits meets the candidate's $100K+ FTE target. NYC-adjusted range ($131K-$148K) is competitive. Equity in a well-funded fintech (Mercury has raised $163M+) could add meaningful upside. Total compensation package likely exceeds minimum requirements.

## Recommendation
Viable but not ideal. The role meets comp targets and engages CS-1 and CS-5, but Mercury wants a technically hands-on TPM who debugs systems alongside engineers — a profile that underutilizes the candidate's enterprise governance, cloud/DevOps, and regulated delivery strengths. Apply only if willing to lean into a more engineering-adjacent TPM identity and de-emphasize portfolio-scale program management.
