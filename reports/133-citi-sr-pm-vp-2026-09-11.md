## Summary

Citi is hiring a Senior Project Manager at the VP level in New York City. VP title signals genuine seniority — this is not a coordinator-level role but a program ownership position within one of the world's largest financial institutions.

- **CS-1 Enterprise Technical Program Delivery** — STRONG. Citi's enterprise scale (200,000+ employees globally) and the VP-level PM scope directly align with Verisk-caliber program delivery. Large budgets, cross-functional teams, and portfolio-level execution are expected at this level.
- **CS-2 Cloud & Data Platform Modernization** — not engaged. Citi PM roles at VP level are typically domain-agnostic program management. No AWS/Snowflake signals. -0.3 applies.
- **CS-3 DevOps Delivery Governance** — not engaged. Financial services PMs operate under change management frameworks, but the DevOps-specific governance (blue/green, immutable infra, CI/CD) is unlikely to be the focus.
- **CS-4 Executive Reporting (Power BI)** — not engaged. Citi has its own internal reporting tools; Power BI expertise is unlikely to be a differentiator.
- **CS-5 Cross-Functional Stakeholder Alignment in Regulated Environments** — PARTIAL. Banking regulatory environment (OCC, Fed, SOX) is a different flavor of regulated delivery than pharma R&D, but the stakeholder alignment skills transfer. Cross-functional coordination across global banking divisions maps well.
- **CS-6 SAFe Enterprise Agile Transformation (SPC6)** — not explicitly required. Neutral.
- **CS-7 Regulated & Compliance-Aware Delivery** — PARTIAL. Banking compliance (SOX, OCC, internal audit) is structurally similar to pharma R&D governance. CAB experience and change control discipline from Verisk transfer directly.

## Fit — where he wins

- VP title at Citi is a genuine seniority match — this is not a step down from Senior TPM / Delivery Lead positioning
- Enterprise financial services delivery at Citi's scale (global, heavily regulated, multi-billion dollar IT spend) validates CS-1 at the highest level
- Regulated delivery experience (pharma R&D at Sanofi, compliance-aware infrastructure at Verisk) transfers to banking regulatory frameworks
- CAB chairmanship, change control, and release governance from Verisk are directly applicable to Citi's change management rigor
- PMP + PMI-ACP credentials are table stakes at Citi VP level — Erick has both
- NYC location is commutable from NJ (Greater NYC Metro)

## Gaps — where he loses

- CS-2 (Cloud & Data Platform) goes entirely unused — the primary technical differentiator has no surface area in a domain-agnostic VP PM role
- NYC onsite requirement means daily commute from NJ, which conflicts with remote preference
- No direct banking/financial services experience — pharma R&D and insurance analytics are adjacent but not identical regulated domains
- Citi-specific systems, tools, and internal governance frameworks require ramp-up time
- Competition for Citi VP PM roles is intense — candidates with direct banking PM experience will have an advantage
- Power BI dashboards (CS-4) are unlikely to differentiate in Citi's proprietary tooling ecosystem

However, CS-2 — the primary technical differentiator — is absent, which means competing on general PM credentials rather than cloud/data platform delivery expertise.
