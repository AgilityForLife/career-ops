- **Healthcare adjacency.** Sanofi pharma R&D governance provides regulated-environment credibility in a healthcare context.
- **Remote US** — no location conflict.

## Gaps — where he loses

- **"AI-focused" transformation.** Erick's AI experience is limited to BTII instructional context (Claude, ChatGPT, PowerApps). Enterprise-wide AI adoption strategy and process mining are outside his delivery resume. - **CS-2 absent.** No cloud/data platform modernization dimension — the primary technical differentiator does not surface.
