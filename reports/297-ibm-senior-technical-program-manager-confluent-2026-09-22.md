## Role Summary

- **Title:** Senior Technical Program Manager — Confluent (Confluent is an IBM subsidiary; role sits inside Confluent's engineering organization).
- **Locations:** Remote, hiring across the United States.
- **Scope:** Lead high-priority technical initiatives spanning Business Continuity Planning, Disaster Recovery, and cost-optimization programs. Act as technical liaison translating business objectives into technical specifications; set program-management standards (milestones, KPIs); partner with leadership on roadmap prioritization and resource management; facilitate decisions across distributed global teams.
- **Required:** 5+ years TPM experience in cloud-native/large-scale software environments; deep knowledge of distributed systems and cloud platforms (AWS/GCP/Azure); understanding of data-streaming technologies (Apache Kafka); Bachelor's in CS/Engineering or related; cross-functional collaboration with engineering, product, and finance.
- **Preferred:** cost-management tooling in large infrastructure environments; familiarity with Confluent or comparable large-scale platforms; dashboard-building proficiency (JQL, SQL, Data Studio, BigQuery).
- **Comp:** Not listed on this requisition. Market comparables: IBM Senior TPM average base ~$180K (range $164K–$229K total pay, Glassdoor); Confluent Senior TPM average ~$201K (range $163K–$252K, Glassdoor).
