## Resumen

JPMorgan Chase is hiring a Principal Technical Program Manager serving as Chief of Staff to the CDAS (Corporate Data & Analytics Solutions) leadership team. Location is Jersey City, NJ. The COS role serves as a strategic partner and trusted advisor driving operational excellence, strategic initiatives, and organizational effectiveness.

Key responsibilities include: developing and implementing strategic technical program plans aligned with organizational goals; supporting the Head of CDAS in developing, communicating, and executing strategic plans and priorities; acting as a liaison between the Head of CDAS and internal/external stakeholders; ensuring adherence to governance frameworks, compliance standards, and risk management practices; leading or supporting key projects and programs while monitoring milestones, risks, and dependencies; supporting change management efforts related to technology transformation, process enhancements, and organizational restructuring; and championing adoption of data practices and AI/ML tools to advance organizational objectives.

Required: 7+ years in technical program management leading complex technology projects in large organizations. Proven Chief of Staff, program management, or technology leadership experience required.
