## Role Summary

Citi is hiring a Senior Program Manager for the Citi Direct NextGen initiative — the next generation of the flagship CitiDirect product — covering Payments and Electronic Banking Channels business domains, based in Jersey City. The role involves leading program management efforts and partnering with key stakeholders within the TTS (Treasury and Trade Solutions) product organization, service teams, and cross-functional technical teams. The PM ensures all project milestones are delivered on time with quality so that intended client experience improvements are met.

The role requires deep understanding of Agile development methodology and experience leading multiple cross-functional, multi-phase programs. Candidates should have comprehensive understanding of Payments and Electronic Banking Channels business domains, strong analytical and problem-solving skills, solid risk identification and issue management capabilities, and a solid technical background to understand architectural designs and interdependencies between teams. Experience in microservice development is a valuable asset. The program reports frequently to Senior Management within and outside the technology organization.
