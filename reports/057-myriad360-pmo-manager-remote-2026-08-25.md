## Gaps y mitigacion

### 1. People Management of a PM Team (Material)

The role explicitly manages Senior Project Managers, PMs, and Coordinators — recruiting, developing, performance-managing a delivery team. Erick's experience is influence-based program leadership across 15-17 cross-functional teams and coaching ARTs, but his CV shows no direct report ownership of a PM team (hiring, reviews, career development).

**Mitigation:** Frame ART leadership and PM mentoring (Verisk + BTII) as functional people leadership. "Led and mentored delivery teams of 50+ across 15-17 cross-functional workstreams; coached mid-career PMs and Scrum Masters into senior delivery roles." Acknowledge the gap honestly if pressed — the governance, standards, and delivery oversight transfer directly; formal HR-line management is the growth edge.

### 2. Customer-Facing PS Delivery (Moderate)

Myriad360 is a technology solutions provider delivering to external customers. Erick's primary experience is internal enterprise delivery. SOW management, project profitability (margin/utilization), and customer satisfaction as a revenue function are outside his primary lane.

**Mitigation:** Surface DSG (Comcast), SilverSearch (Vitamin Shoppe), and AFR as client-facing delivery engagements. Frame Sanofi's multi-stakeholder R&D program as internal-consulting with executive-client dynamics. Acknowledge that PS profitability discipline (utilization rates, SOW scope management, margin tracking) would require ramp-up.

### 3. Domain Breadth: Data Center, Network & Security, Field Services

These specific technology domains are outside Erick's experience. Cloud aligns; the others do not.

**Mitigation:** At PMO leadership level, the role is about delivery governance and standards, not domain expertise in each practice. Erick's track record of governing diverse workstreams (software engineering, DevOps, infrastructure, DBA, UI/UX, mainframe at Verisk) demonstrates the ability to oversee heterogeneous technology portfolios without being a domain expert in each.

## Decision

However, two material gaps prevent a higher score:
1. **People management of a PM team** — Erick has never held direct-report authority over project managers. Influence-based leadership at scale is adjacent but not equivalent.
2. **Customer-facing PS delivery** — Myriad360 delivers to external customers; Erick's primary experience is internal enterprise programs. SOW/margin/utilization management is outside his core.

**Classification: SECONDARY TARGET ROLE.** Worth preparing an application if Erick is willing to invest in bridging the people-management and PS-delivery narratives. Not a reach application — the governance alignment is genuine — but the gaps are real enough to flag.
