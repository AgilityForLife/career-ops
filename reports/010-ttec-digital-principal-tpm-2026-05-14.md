## Role Summary

TTEC Digital is hiring a Principal Technical Project Manager for their NICE CXone Practice. The role provides program and project management services for design, deployment, and delivery support of contact center solutions in a client-facing consulting environment.

**Comp:** $120,000–$135,000 + annual incentive
**Location:** Remote (NJ/NY eligible, some client site travel)

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Lead implementation of large enterprise projects and small programs (teams 3–30)
- Provide frequent contact with senior managers, directors, and executives
- Set up and drive program management functions: communication plans, tracking, reporting, escalation
- Deliver weekly status reports and financial reporting dashboards
- Manage overall delivery relationship with assigned clients
- Ensure client stakeholder alignment and program customer satisfaction

**Requirements:**
- 5+ years PM experience in consulting environment
- 3-5 years strategic development with significant client impact
- Experience leading teams (3-30 people)
- Hands-on experience with formal methodology deliverables
- Strong written and verbal communication
