## Role Summary

Citi is hiring a Technical Program Manager at the SVP level in Jersey City to handle key programs and projects for regulatory, risk, and control-related initiatives in the Data and Analytics space. The role specifically involves managing the D2R2 platform — a new risk platform that risk-ranks all controls metrics at the exception level, helping the Operations & Controls organization identify the riskiest items — and PRA (Prudential Regulation Authority) regulatory programs. The TPM manages the Book of Work discussions, stakeholder expectations, and governance with Operations stakeholders for the regulatory area.

The position requires a motivated and organized senior manager with substantial experience in developing and managing applications in the Risk, Control, and operations domain, managing multiple firm-critical programs, and working closely with business stakeholders and technology partners on requirement analysis and solution design. Salary range: $164,310 - $246,460 annually.
