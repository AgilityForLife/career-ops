## Summary

Deloitte is seeking a Technical Program Manager to lead large-scale retail store transformation programs, including POS modernization, in-store technology deployment, and board-level executive reporting. The role demands 8+ years of retail-specific POS modernization experience and involves managing multi-market store rollouts across hundreds or thousands of locations. Compensation is strong ($140K-$180K + overtime + incentive) and the program delivery archetype aligns well with CS-1, but the retail domain requirement is a hard gap.

## Core Strength Engagement Test

- **CS-1 Enterprise Technical Program Delivery** — STRONG. Large-scale store transformation across multiple markets is enterprise program delivery by any definition. Multi-year roadmaps, cross-functional team coordination, and budget governance at Deloitte consulting scale map directly to Verisk experience.
- **CS-2 Cloud & Data Platform Modernization** — not engaged. Retail POS modernization is hardware/software deployment, not cloud platform migration. -0.3 applies.
- **CS-3 DevOps Delivery Governance** — PARTIAL. Deployment governance across hundreds of store locations is a different flavor of release/deployment management. Blue/green and immutable infrastructure don't apply, but phased rollout governance, change control, and deployment readiness do.
- **CS-4 Executive Reporting (Power BI)** — ENGAGED. Board-level and executive reporting is explicitly called out. Power BI dashboard experience for VP/executive stakeholders at Verisk and Sanofi transfers directly. +0.2 bonus applies.
- **CS-5 Cross-Functional Stakeholder Alignment** — PARTIAL. Multi-market coordination across store operations, IT, vendors, and executive leadership requires the same cross-functional alignment skills demonstrated at Verisk and Sanofi.
- **CS-6 SAFe Enterprise Agile Transformation (SPC6)** — not explicitly required. Neutral.
- **CS-7 Regulated & Compliance-Aware Delivery** — not engaged. Retail is not a regulated environment in the pharma/banking sense.
