## Role Summary

Citi is seeking a Senior Program/Project Manager at the VP level for NAM (North America) Issuer Services Technology in Jersey City. The role focuses on executing and leading NAM and global projects and programs spanning across all Issuer Services applications and business lines, with a focus on transformation, modernization, and innovation. Key responsibilities include creating, managing, and reporting on key milestones; budget and capacity planning; executive management reporting; daily program management throughout the lifecycle of transformation programs; and creating a robust project governance structure for the Issuer Services technology group.

The Issuer Services Technology team manages a suite of business-critical applications globally supporting Agency & Trust and Depositary Receipts business Operations and Front Office teams. The role involves day-to-day management of regulatory and business mandatory enterprise enhancements, working closely with the global technology management team, NAM technology teams, and various stakeholder levels.
