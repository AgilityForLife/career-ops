## Role Summary

Accela is a cloud-based civic technology platform serving government agencies. The Technical Program Manager role involves leading complex cross-functional programs spanning engineering, data, and technology initiatives. The position requires translating technical initiatives into clear execution plans, managing dependencies, risks, and timelines, and partnering with engineering, product, and business stakeholders. The role is remote-first, US-based, with a salary range of $140,000–$160,000. Agile delivery experience and PMP/Agile/Scrum certifications are listed as a plus.

## Fit Analysis

### Strengths

1. **Enterprise program delivery (CS-1) is the core of this role.** Leading complex cross-functional programs, managing dependencies and risks, and translating technical initiatives into execution plans — this is Erick's primary differentiator with Verisk-scale evidence (5 teams, 50+ engineers, $3M budget).

2. **Government/regulated environment (CS-7) is a natural fit.** Accela serves government agencies, which means procurement compliance, data privacy, accessibility standards, and audit requirements. Erick's regulated delivery experience at Sanofi (pharma R&D governance) and Verisk (compliance-aware infrastructure) directly transfers to civic tech.

3. **Cross-functional stakeholder alignment (CS-5) is explicitly required.** Partnering with engineering, product, and business stakeholders mirrors Erick's track record coordinating across data engineering, MDM, analytics, architecture, and business teams at Sanofi (US + Europe).

4. **Certifications are a differentiator.** PMP, PMI-ACP, and SPC6 are listed as "a plus" — Erick holds all three, which is rare and signals credentialed program discipline beyond what most candidates bring.

5. **Compensation is strong.** $140–160K FTE significantly exceeds the $100K floor and lands within the target range ($130–150K). This role unlocks premium-band comp.

6. **Remote-first matches preferences.** No relocation, no mandatory on-site — aligns with deal-breaker requirements.

### Gaps

1. **No specific cloud/data stack alignment (CS-2 partial).** The JD does not mention AWS, Snowflake, Terraform, or any specific infrastructure. While Accela is cloud-based, the role appears more program-management-focused than platform-delivery-focused. Erick's AWS + Snowflake depth may be underutilized.

2. **No DevOps governance signals (CS-3 absent).** No mention of CI/CD, release governance, CAB processes, or infrastructure automation. The 40% outage reduction and 70% provisioning improvement stories may not have a natural landing point.

3. **No executive reporting/BI requirement (CS-4 absent).** No mention of Power BI, dashboards, or data-driven reporting. This eliminates the +0.2 bonus and removes one of Erick's strongest differentiators from the conversation.

4. **Government software domain is new.** While regulated delivery experience transfers, civic technology / GovTech has its own procurement cycles (RFP-driven), compliance frameworks, and stakeholder dynamics that differ from pharma and financial services.

5. **JD is broad, not deep.** The requirements are somewhat generic ("technical fluency," "excellent communication") without specifying scale, team size, or budget ownership. This could mean the role is lighter-weight than Erick's senior TPM experience, or it could mean the JD is underwritten.
