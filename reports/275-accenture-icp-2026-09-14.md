## Role Summary

Accenture Infrastructure & Capital Projects (ICP) is hiring a Senior Project Manager within their Advisory & Network Services (ANS) practice. The position requires 10+ years of direct PM experience on electric transmission, substation, distribution, and/or comparable heavy civil/utility infrastructure projects. PG&E (Pacific Gas & Electric) experience is strongly preferred. Tools include MS Project and Primavera P6.
