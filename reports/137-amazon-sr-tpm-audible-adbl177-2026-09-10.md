## Gaps — where he loses

- **Hands-on Java/C# / J2EE / MVC / Web Services (5+ years) is a stated basic qualification.** Per `cv.md`, Erick has no direct hands-on software engineering/coding background at this depth — his technical delivery experience is program/platform-level (AWS infrastructure, Snowflake data platform, CI/CD), not application-level coding. This is a real, hard requirement gap not captured by the CS-framework weighting and is the single biggest risk to clearing an ATS/recruiter screen.
- **CS-2 (cloud/data platform) is absent from the JD.** Erick's strongest technical differentiator (AWS + Snowflake delivery) has no explicit hook here, even though it's real and can still be surfaced as generally relevant enterprise-platform experience.
- **CS-4 (Power BI executive reporting)** — no explicit reporting/dashboard requirement, so this differentiator won't land as directly as in a data-platform-forward role.
- **"Fixed location-based with possible telecommuting"** is ambiguous — not a confirmed 4–5 day mandatory onsite (not treated as an automatic deal-breaker per profile.yml), but also not confirmed remote/hybrid-flexible. Worth clarifying before/during interview.
- **CS-7 (regulated delivery)** doesn't clearly transfer — Audible/media content isn't the same regulatory context as pharma R&D or financial services, even though Infosec involvement is named.
