## Gaps — where he loses

- **GTM/revenue-operations domain experience is hard-required, not preferred.** The JD explicitly requires "hands-on experience with GTM processes: lead-to-cash, quote-to-cash, renewal/expansion, customer health." Erick has zero lead-to-cash or quote-to-cash ownership. This is the load-bearing requirement he cannot credibly clear.
- **CPQ, Deal Desk, and customer health tooling** are specialist domains. Erick's AFR Wholesale Salesforce CRM delivery (~500 users, ~$5M annual revenue, 20% time-to-market reduction) is the closest adjacent proof point, but it was contract/order-management at a different company scale, not CPQ pipeline management at an AI company scaling its enterprise sales motion.
- **Revenue operations is a different muscle from technical program delivery.** RevOps TPMs live in the commercial pipeline — pipeline hygiene, bookings forecasts, renewal workflows, expansion triggers. Erick's career has been in engineering/platform delivery, not commercial-systems delivery. The structural overlap (coordination, stakeholder alignment, dependency management) is real; the domain expertise (CPQ configuration, deal desk workflows, customer health scoring) is not.
- **CS-2, his primary technical differentiator, is unused here.** AWS, Snowflake, cloud infrastructure — none of it surfaces.
- **This is Anthropic's third evaluated role (after 039 Enterprise Commerce at 3.6/5 and 041 Cloud Inference at 3.2/5).** Enterprise Commerce is the materially better fit — it shares the quote-to-cash domain but adds SOX readiness and enterprise systems integration where Erick has real evidence. This role is a weaker version of the same domain ask.
