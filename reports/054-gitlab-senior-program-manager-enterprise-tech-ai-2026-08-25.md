## Gaps — where he loses

- **AI program depth is limited.** The role sits in an "Enterprise Technology & AI" team designing "AI-enabled workflows." Erick's AI experience is constrained to BTII instructional context (Claude, ChatGPT, PowerApps, Power Automate). He has not led enterprise AI program delivery — AI adoption coaching is not AI platform operations. Per skills-model AI-Adjacent PM framing: bridge as "applying program delivery discipline to AI adoption and tooling," but do not overclaim.
- **Enterprise IT / business systems domain.** The JD references "business systems, data, AI, security, infrastructure, enterprise architecture" as Enterprise IT domains. Erick's delivery experience is product/platform-oriented (Verisk analytics products, Sanofi R&D data foundation) rather than internal corporate IT (ERP, CRM at enterprise scale, ITSM, identity management, IT procurement). Jira/Power BI/ServiceNow tooling provides partial coverage, but this is a domain gap.
- **No direct SaaS product company PM experience.** GitLab is a developer-tools SaaS company. Erick's experience is in financial services (Verisk) and pharma (Sanofi). The internal IT focus mitigates this somewhat — this role is not product PM — but cultural fluency with SaaS-native engineering organizations may be tested.
- **"Enterprise Architecture" familiarity.** Listed as a domain. Erick's architecture involvement has been at the delivery level (golden AMIs, EKS/ECS, Terraform modules, Snowflake schemas) rather than enterprise architecture practice (TOGAF, reference architectures, technology roadmaps as an EA function).
- **CIO org context.** Reporting within a CIO organization means internal IT delivery — optimizing the company's own technology stack, not building products for customers. This is a different operating model than Erick's product-delivery background. The organizational dynamics, budget justification, and stakeholder relationships differ.

These are real but not disqualifying — the role emphasizes program management craft over domain expertise, and the JD frames Enterprise IT familiarity as preferred, not required.

**Classification: SECONDARY TARGET ROLE** — Enterprise IT/AI program management vs. Erick's product/platform delivery background. Worth the application given comp, remote fit, and company prestige. HELD for manual review.
