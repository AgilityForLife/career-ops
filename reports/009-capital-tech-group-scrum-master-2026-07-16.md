## Job Description Summary

CTG is seeking a Scrum Master to support a mission-critical identity and access management (IAM) program serving U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS). The program manages how every employee, contractor, and partner agency logs in and what they can access. The role involves facilitating Agile ceremonies in two-week sprint cycles, partnering with product owners, BAs, engineers, QA, DevOps, and program leadership, and coordinating across multiple delivery teams.
