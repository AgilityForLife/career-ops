## Company Overview

Life360 is the leading family safety and location-sharing platform with approximately 97.8 million monthly active users across 170+ countries. The company operates a remote-first model with 500+ employees distributed across the US and Canada. Life360's product suite spans real-time location sharing, crash detection, driving safety, and digital safety for families. The company is publicly traded (NASDAQ: LIF) and has grown significantly through acquisitions (Tile, Jiobit). The AI Native designation in this role signals a strategic organizational push to embed AI into core workflows, not just product features.

## Role Summary

This is a Staff-level TPM role focused on upleveling the operating system that the broader group runs on. Core responsibilities include quarterly planning, strategic goal tracking and measurement, key reviews, change management, and program execution. The role works hands-on across engineering, product, marketing, and design. A distinguishing element is the explicit AI-native transformation mandate: moving the team from AI-assisted workflows to genuinely AI-native ones. First-year success criteria are clear and measurable — department planning runs reliably, and quarterly cycles are predictable and trusted by VPs.

The role reports into a group that spans multiple functions and requires someone who can operate as a strategic program operator, not just a delivery coordinator.

Life360 is a consumer product company (family safety, location sharing, 97.8M MAU). The planning rhythms, stakeholder dynamics, and success metrics in consumer tech differ from enterprise — faster cycles, more marketing-driven priorities, user growth metrics over revenue enablement.

- **"Staff" level ambiguity.** Staff TPM at a 500-person company may imply IC seniority with broad influence but no direct reports — which fits. However, it could also imply deep technical depth in AI/ML systems that the candidate does not possess.
