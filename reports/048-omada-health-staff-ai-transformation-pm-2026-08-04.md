## Company Overview

Omada Health is a digital-first chronic care management company that combines clinical protocols, behavioral science, and connected devices to treat conditions such as diabetes, hypertension, and musculoskeletal pain. The company works with employers and health plans, operating in a regulated healthcare environment (HIPAA, clinical data governance). Omada has raised significant venture funding and serves hundreds of enterprise clients. The role reports to the VP of IT, signaling that AI transformation sits within the technology organization, not a standalone innovation lab.

## Role Summary

Senior individual-contributor role owning the enterprise-wide AI Transformation and Business Process Excellence program. The Staff PM will build and run governance models, intake processes, and prioritization frameworks for AI-driven operational improvements across the organization. Key deliverables include executive reporting on transformation progress, delivery oversight for complex cross-functional initiatives, and workflow optimization at enterprise scale. The role demands independent ownership in ambiguous environments, strong analytical rigor, and executive communication skills. This is a transformation-leadership seat, not a technical ML/AI engineering role.

## Fit Analysis

### Strengths

- **Enterprise program ownership is the job.** The entire role is CS-1: own the AI Transformation program, build governance, drive delivery of cross-functional initiatives. Verisk (15-17 teams, $3M budget, 4 releases/year) is direct proof of this at comparable or larger scale.
- **Executive reporting is called out explicitly.** The JD names executive reporting, governance dashboards, and analytics-driven prioritization. Erick's Power BI executive dashboards at Verisk and Sanofi (built for VP/R&D leadership decisions) are a rare differentiator most PM candidates cannot demonstrate.
- **Transformation governance maps to SPC6 discipline.** Standing up intake processes, prioritization frameworks, and governance models for a new AI transformation program mirrors the Lean Portfolio Management work at Verisk (embedding LPM, Jira Align portfolio structures, value stream identification).
- **Cross-functional alignment in regulated environments.** Sanofi (pharma R&D, US + Europe, data engineering + MDM + analytics + architecture + business stakeholders) demonstrates exactly the cross-org coordination this role demands, in a similarly regulated healthcare-adjacent context.
- **BTII AI work is directly relevant here (rare).** The BTII experience (AI-driven business process automation using Claude, ChatGPT, PowerApps, Power Automate, Copilot) is normally de-emphasized, but this role explicitly targets AI transformation and business process excellence -- making BTII's AI adoption and operational framework work a legitimate proof point. Surface it selectively.
- **"Ambiguous environments" tolerance.** Standing up a new transformation office is inherently ambiguous. Verisk's waterfall-to-SAFe transformation and Sanofi's greenfield Snowflake migration both demonstrate comfort operating without a pre-existing playbook.

### Gaps

- **No cloud/data platform engineering scope (CS-2 absent).** Erick's primary technical differentiator (AWS infrastructure, Snowflake OneMesh, Terraform, CI/CD delivery) is not what this role rewards. The "AI" in the title refers to business process transformation via AI tools, not platform delivery. This is a -0.3 scoring factor and means the resume cannot lead with cloud/data credentials.
- **No DevOps governance scope (CS-3 absent).** CAB, release management, blue/green deployments, and incident postmortems are irrelevant here. Neutral -- not a gap the role would notice.
- **AI/ML depth not demonstrated.** While the role is not an ML engineering seat, candidates with deeper AI strategy experience (model evaluation, AI ethics/governance frameworks, vendor selection for LLMs) may have an edge. Erick's BTII AI work is practical but not strategic-level AI program management. Mitigate by framing as "applying enterprise program discipline to AI adoption" per the interview prep rules for AI-Adjacent PM roles.
- **Healthcare domain is new.** No direct healthcare delivery experience. Sanofi (pharma R&D) is the closest bridge -- pharma and digital health share regulatory awareness, data sensitivity, and clinical stakeholder complexity. Not a hard blocker but worth addressing proactively.
- **Compensation band may compress.** $155K-$202K is FTE. At the lower tier (non-CA/NY/WA), $155K is above Erick's $100K floor and within the $130-150K target range. At the higher tier (NY), $185-202K would be above target. No comp concern.
